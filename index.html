ASIAN GAMES

India beat Pakistan by 19 runs to win Gold

India posted 211/6 in 20 overs before restricting Pakistan to 192/6. India secured the men's cricket gold medal at the Asian Games.

PAKISTAN

Hasan Nawaz hits fighting 96

Hasan Nawaz scored a brilliant 96 from 51 balls, including five fours and seven sixes, but Pakistan fell short in the final.

INDIA

Abhishek Sharma stars with 61

Abhishek Sharma scored 61 from 28 balls while Tilak Varma finished unbeaten on 51 from 22 balls as India reached 211/6.

PAKISTAN

Pakistan finish Asian Games with Silver

Pakistan claimed the silver medal after their 19-run defeat against India in the men's cricket final.

CRICKET

Sri Lanka take Bronze Medal

Sri Lanka defeated Bangladesh by 63 runs in the third-place playoff to claim the bronze medal.
